# Homework: Competency 1.2

A community group records 600 relief-item movements each day across three locations. Connectivity sometimes fails.

1. Explain two limitations of a paper-only method at this scale.
2. Recommend a connectivity-aware digital or hybrid workflow and justify three features, including how work continues offline.
3. Explain one way the proposed technology could make an error spread faster.
4. Propose two controls for reliability or accuracy.
5. Explain whether viewing the group’s linked public updates is use of the Internet, the Web, or both.
6. Distinguish the mobile computing, mobile communication, and cloud computing used in the workflow. Then state one safety or security risk and a practical control.
