# Why Information Work Needs Technology

## Question

When does a manual information method stop being fit for purpose?

## Bulk manual work

Consider elapsed time, repetition, transcription, consistency, checking, storage, retrieval, and dissemination.

## Technology changes the workflow

Electronic processing can automate repeated rules, reproduce calculations, store records, and distribute results—but controls still matter.

## Internet ≠ World Wide Web

- Internet: interconnected network infrastructure and protocols.
- Web: linked resources accessed as a service over the Internet.
- Email and file transfer also use the Internet without being the Web.

## Mobile computing

Portable processing + communication + work across locations.

Check connectivity, battery, security, screen/input limits, and offline recovery.

## Decision rule

Choose a method from requirements and evidence, not from novelty.

## Exit prompt

Name one task where a manual method remains appropriate and justify it.
