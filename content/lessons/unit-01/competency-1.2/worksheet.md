# Worksheet: Selecting technology for information work

1. Explain how increasing record volume can affect the speed of a manual workflow.
2. Distinguish unreliability from inaccuracy using one example each.
3. Explain why automating an incorrect rule does not improve information quality.
4. Classify each as Internet infrastructure, a Web activity, or another Internet service: router forwarding a packet; opening a linked page; sending email; following a hyperlink.
5. Explain the relationship between the Internet and the World Wide Web.
6. A mobile survey team works where connectivity is intermittent. State two benefits, two constraints, and two controls.
7. A club processes twelve membership renewals per year. Recommend a manual, digital, or hybrid method and justify it against scale, accuracy, retrieval, and cost.
8. A clinic must distribute an urgent public notice widely. Explain how merging information and communication technologies helps, without using patient data.
