# Answers and marking guidance

## Worksheet

1. Data: the individual values have not yet been summarised for the manager’s stated task. (1)
2. Information: the values were added to answer a daily-target question. (1)
3. Data of poor quality: without unit, time, and location its meaning is incomplete. (2)
4. Information: it is contextualised and supports the traveller’s decision. (1)
5. Monday 7, Tuesday 10, Wednesday 9. (3)
6. Login 9, Printing 8, Network 9. (3)
7. Award one mark per supported claim and one per cited value. Examples: Tuesday had the most requests (10); printing requests peaked Tuesday (5); network requests peaked Wednesday (6). (4)
8. Any two: addition, grouping, comparison, identifying a maximum, summarising. (2)
9. Any three: missing date/time, class, issuer, original room or effective period. (3)
10. One plausible consequence per problem, linked clearly. (3)
11. Award for date/time, intended class, clear change/location, and issuer. (4)
12. Context, purpose, or prior processing changes whether the record answers the user’s question. A valid example is required. (3)
13. It may be irrelevant, late, incomplete for the decision, unclear, or inaccessible despite being correct. (2)
14. The figures may be accurate and complete but are not timely; current usage or enrolment data should be collected or combined with the historical trend. (3)

Worksheet total: 35 marks.

## Practical

Total 110; mean 22; highest Thursday 27; lowest Monday 18. Accept any accurate chart and claims limited to the recorded week. A valid limitation is that one week cannot establish a stable future pattern or explain why enquiries occurred.

## Homework

1. Mean = `(32 + 28 + 35 + 30 + 25) / 5 = 30` minutes. (2)
2. Example: the mean recorded delivery time was 30 minutes. (1)
3. The five observations were summed and divided by five. (2)
4. The mean meets the target exactly, but two of five deliveries exceeded 30 minutes; the mean alone hides variation. (3)
5. Any two non-identifying fields such as distance band, time of day, traffic condition, vehicle type, or delivery zone. (2)
6. Accuracy is correctness relative to reality; relevance is usefulness for the stated purpose. Award one mark for each explanation and one for a valid contrasting example. (3)

Homework total: 13 marks.
