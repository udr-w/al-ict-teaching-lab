# Worksheet: Data and Information

## A. Distinguish in context

For each item, state whether it functions as data or information **for the stated user and purpose**, and justify your answer.

1. A shop manager sees the individual sale values `1200, 850, 1500, 950` before preparing the daily summary.
2. The same manager sees “Total sales today: Rs 4,500” when deciding whether the daily target was met.
3. A weather station stores the symbol `28.6` without its unit, time, or location.
4. A traveller reads “The 07:10 bus is delayed by 20 minutes” while choosing a route.

## B. Create information

A fictional computer laboratory recorded the following number of support requests:

| Day | Login | Printing | Network |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Monday | 4 | 2 | 1 |
| Tuesday | 3 | 5 | 2 |
| Wednesday | 2 | 1 | 6 |

5. Calculate the total requests for each day.
6. Calculate the total for each request type.
7. State two information claims useful to the technician. Cite the values supporting each claim.
8. Name two manipulations you performed.

## C. Evaluate quality

A notice says: “The ICT practical has moved to Room 4.” It contains no date, class, or issuing teacher.

9. Identify three quality problems.
10. Explain one likely consequence of each problem.
11. Rewrite the notice using fictional details so it is fit for purpose.

## D. Individual check

12. Explain why the same record may be data for one user but information for another.
13. Explain why accurate information may have low value.
14. A report uses complete and accurate figures from three years ago to allocate computers next week. Evaluate its quality and recommend one improvement.
