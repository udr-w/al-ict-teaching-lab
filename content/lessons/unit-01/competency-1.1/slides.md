# From Data to Useful Information

## Today’s question

When do recorded facts become useful information?

## Learning outcomes

- Distinguish data from information in context.
- Explain how data manipulation creates information.
- Evaluate information quality and value.

## Notice and wonder

`31, 27, 29, 35, 30`

What might these values represent? What can we claim before receiving context?

## Data and information

- Data: recorded observations or symbols.
- Information: data interpreted or processed for a user and purpose.
- Context determines meaning.

## Common transformations

Sort · classify · calculate · compare · summarise · visualise

Every transformation should help answer a stated question.

## Quality check

Is it accurate, complete, relevant, timely, consistent, clear, and fit for purpose?

## Value depends on context

Ask: useful to whom, for what decision, and at what time?

## Worked example

Fictional help-desk requests: Mon 12, Tue 18, Wed 15.

Total = 45; mean = 15 per day; Tuesday has the highest recorded demand.

Which statements are calculations, and which are interpretations?

## Exit reflection

Give one example of accurate information that would still be of little value because it is irrelevant or late.
