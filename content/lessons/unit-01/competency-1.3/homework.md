# Homework: Competency 1.3

A fictional water tank sensor reports litres every ten minutes. A warning is needed when the level falls below 500 litres.

1. Construct a specific input–process–output model.
2. State two validation or exception conditions the abstract model should acknowledge.
3. Explain why a warning delivered two days later has low value even if accurate.
4. Evaluate whether a computer is appropriate, giving two benefits and two limitations or controls.
5. Explain one part of the task that may still require human judgement.
